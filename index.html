Safety Policy

At Heartifo, safety is an important part of every ride. This policy explains the basic safety expectations for Customers and Captains using the Heartifo platform.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Safety First

Heartifo encourages Customers and Captains to prioritize safety throughout the entire ride.

  • Follow applicable traffic and road safety rules.
  • Wear appropriate safety equipment such as helmets where required.
  • Do not use the service in a dangerous or unsafe manner.
  • Follow reasonable instructions from authorities and emergency personnel.

2. Captain Safety Responsibilities

Captains using Heartifo are expected to:

  • Maintain their vehicle in a reasonably safe condition.
  • Follow applicable driving and traffic regulations.
  • Carry valid documents required for operating the vehicle.
  • Drive responsibly and avoid dangerous driving behavior.
  • Never drive while under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Treat Customers respectfully.

3. Customer Safety Responsibilities

Customers are expected to:

  • Provide accurate pickup and destination information.
  • Follow reasonable safety instructions from the Captain.
  • Use safety equipment provided or required by law.
  • Behave respectfully toward Captains.
  • Do not intentionally damage or misuse the vehicle.

4. Prohibited Safety Behavior

The following behavior is not permitted on the Heartifo platform:

  • Threats, violence, harassment, or intimidation.
  • Sexual harassment or inappropriate conduct.
  • Dangerous or reckless driving.
  • Use of illegal drugs during a ride.
  • Carrying prohibited or illegal items.
  • Attempting to misuse another person's account.
  • Any behavior that creates a serious safety risk.

5. Emergency Situations

If an emergency occurs during a ride, Customers and Captains should first move to a safe location when possible and contact the appropriate emergency services.

Important: Heartifo is not a replacement for emergency services. In an immediate emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency authority.

6. Reporting a Safety Concern

Customers and Captains can report safety concerns, inappropriate behavior, accidents, or other serious issues to Heartifo.

  • Provide the ride details when possible.
  • Explain what happened clearly.
  • Provide relevant information or evidence if available.

Heartifo may review reports and take appropriate action based on the available information.

7. Account Safety

Users are responsible for keeping their account information secure. Do not share OTPs, passwords, or other authentication information with another person.

Never share your Heartifo OTP with anyone, including someone claiming to be a Heartifo representative.

8. Safety Review and Enforcement

Heartifo may review safety-related complaints and take appropriate action where necessary. Depending on the circumstances, this may include warnings, temporary restrictions, suspension, or termination of an account, subject to applicable law and platform policies.

9. Privacy and Safety

Heartifo may use relevant ride and account information to investigate safety complaints, prevent misuse, and support the safety of the platform, in accordance with the Heartifo Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

Heartifo may update this Safety Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, safety practices, or applicable requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page.

Need Help?

If you have a safety concern or need assistance, contact the Heartifo support team.
